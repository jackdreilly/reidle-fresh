Privacy Policy

Last updated October 24, 2026

This policy explains what information Reidle (the word game at reidle.reillybrothers.net, run by Reilly Brothers, "we") collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using Reidle you agree to this policy.

What we collect

  • Account details. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. If you sign in with email, we receive your email address. Guests get an anonymous account with no email.
  • What you add to the game. Your nickname, your games (guesses, times and scores), leagues you create or join, chat messages, reactions and challenges.
  • Usage and device information. Pages you visit in the app, how long they are on screen, your browser and device type, approximate settings such as dark mode, and error reports when something breaks. We use this to keep the game working and to improve it.
  • Push notifications. If you turn them on, we store the push subscription your browser gives us so we can send you notifications.

We do not collect payment information, and we do not ask Google for access to your contacts, files or anything beyond your basic profile and email.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To run the game: scores, rankings, leagues, chat and notifications.
  • To send emails you ask for, such as sign-in codes.
  • To fix bugs, understand how the game is used, and improve it.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Your nickname, games, scores and chat messages are visible to other players in the leagues you belong to (and, for the public Reidle Global league, to other players there). Your email address is not shown to other players.

Service providers

We use a few companies to run Reidle. They process information only on our behalf:

  • Supabase hosts our database and handles sign-in.
  • Cloudflare serves the website and provides privacy-friendly, cookie-free web analytics.
  • Google provides "Sign in with Google".
  • Resend sends our emails.
  • GIPHY supplies GIFs when you search for one in chat; your search text is sent to GIPHY.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our players or the service.

Cookies and local storage

Reidle keeps your sign-in session and preferences in your browser's local storage. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while your account exists. You can ask us to delete your account and its data at any time by contacting us (below); we will do so within 30 days, except where we need to keep something to meet a legal obligation.

Security

Your data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with our providers, who use industry-standard protections. No service is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

Reidle is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your information, or to object to how we use it. Contact us to make a request.

Changes

We may update this policy. If the changes are significant we will let players know in the app. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions or requests: reidle@reillybrothers.net.
